# Charitology master plan

All 66 items from the original Claude list are preserved in [the original brief](docs/ORIGINAL_BRIEF.md) and mapped in [the live roadmap](https://charitology.org/roadmap/). The roadmap reads each page’s current status at build time; it is the current record of what exists. The later system-focused frame and STYLE.md govern every item.

A live tool may implement a narrower, safer version of the initial idea. Seeded means starter material. Designed means a charter or method, not an operating service. Partners means people, funding, governance or institutional work is still needed. Human review is distinct from availability.

## Additional operational tools

- [Charity OS](https://charitology.org/tools/charity-os/): local projects, grants, tasks and outcome records. Multiuser accounts, cloud sync, accounting and payment processing are outside this browser workspace.
- [Tools for charities](https://charitology.org/for-charities/tools/): an official-source directory with categories and stated limits.
- [Benefits for charities](https://charitology.org/for-charities/benefits/): an official-source directory with eligibility and application links. Offers must be rechecked before applying.

## Work that cannot be claimed complete by publishing a website

Human source reviews, a legal fund, paid fellowships, cash bounties, prizes, an accredited credential, field audits, a university network, a conference and locally coauthored country profiles need their stated people, resources and governance. The site publishes those commitments openly instead of pretending those institutions exist. Charity ratings remain gated on public red-team review.
