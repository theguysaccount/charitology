## Background

Two of the three wild poliovirus types have been certified eradicated. Wild poliovirus type 1 still circulates in a small number of places. Certification requires several years with no detected wild cases, so a forecast here is a forecast about the end of transmission years earlier.

## Why it matters

Eradication is the clearest case of source-level work in health: once a disease is gone, its symptoms never need funding again. See the [Historical Case Studies](/record/case-studies/).
