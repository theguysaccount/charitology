## Background

Two of the three wild poliovirus types have been certified eradicated. Wild poliovirus type 1 still circulates in a small number of places. Certification requires several years with no detected wild cases, so a forecast here is a forecast about the end of transmission years earlier.

## Why it matters

Charitology describes eradication as source-level work in health because it ends transmission. Certification of wild poliovirus type 1 would not by itself end all polio work: vaccine-derived outbreaks, surveillance and post-certification safeguards also matter.
