## Background

The [Silence About Failure](/diagnosis/silence-about-failure/) is a documented condition of the charity system: publishing a failure can cost funding, so most failures go unpublished. The [Failure Archive](/record/failure-archive/) invites organizations to publish their own.

## Why it matters

If organizations publish voluntarily, the incentive problem may be smaller than feared. That would be early evidence on [Open Problem 9, the Failure Commons](/open-problems/the-failure-commons/).
