## Background

The Annual DAF Report 2025 estimated donor-advised fund assets at $326.45 billion for fiscal year 2024, up 27.5 percent from $255.99 billion in fiscal year 2023. These figures combine sponsors with different fiscal year-end dates; they are not a single calendar-year-end snapshot. Assets change with contributions, grants, investment returns and expenses.

## Why it matters

This tracks one side of [Parked Money](/diagnosis/parked-money/): how much has been given away for tax purposes but has not yet reached working charities.
