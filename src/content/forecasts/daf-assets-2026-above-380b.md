## Background

The Annual DAF Report 2025 put donor-advised fund assets at $326 billion for 2024, up from about $250 billion the year before. Assets grow with new contributions and market returns, and shrink with grants.

## Why it matters

This tracks one side of [Parked Money](/diagnosis/parked-money/): how much has been given away for tax purposes but has not yet reached working charities.
