## Background

Giving USA 2026 estimated total U.S. giving at $617.2 billion for 2025, up 5.7 percent in current dollars. Growth of about 3.7 percent in 2026 would put the total at $640 billion. Market returns, inflation and large bequests all move the number from year to year.

## Why it matters

Total giving is the system’s headline number. Charitology’s argument is that the total matters less than where it goes, and this question tracks the baseline.
