## How it works

Part 1 asks where you would aim: at people alive now or the future, at humans or all life, near or far, at proven results or long shots, at relief or at sources. Part 2 asks how much each of eight moral statements sounds like you. Each statement stands for a framework: welfare, rights and duties, justice, care, faith and tradition, future generations, all life, and flourishing.

Your answers in part 2 become weights on those frameworks. Each framework ranks the [cause areas](/record/cause-atlas/) differently, and your weights combine those rankings. Your answers in part 1 are compared with where each cause sits on the same five dimensions. A cause’s match score is about two-thirds lens and one-third fit.

## What your other lenses see

Most tools tell you what you already believe. The compass also shows the causes your least-used frameworks rank highly, because seeing past one lens is the whole point of charitology. That is the treatment for [the Single Lens](/diagnosis/single-lens/).

## What it isn’t

It isn’t a recommendation to give to any charity, and it isn’t a measurement. The cause positions and framework priorities are editorial judgments, published openly so you can check and challenge them. Your answers stay in your browser. A share link stores them in the link itself, not on any server.
