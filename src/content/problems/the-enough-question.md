## Why it matters

Humanity’s Checklist points out that hundreds of billions of dollars go to charity every year, and asks what would happen if $5 million, spent correctly, were enough to go after the six sources upstream of every world problem. Charitology treats that as a hypothesis to test, not a slogan. If money really isn’t the bottleneck, the field should find out what is.

## Where to start

Pick one problem, write the source-level plan, cost it, fund it and publish what happens, including if it fails.
