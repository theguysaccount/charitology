## Why it matters

[Fragmentation](/diagnosis/fragmentation/) is easy to see and hard to judge. A plural sector experiments more. A fragmented one may waste more. The answer probably depends on the field.

## Where to start

Nonprofit mergers, shared back-office services and collective-impact coalitions all leave records. Outcome data before and after is the missing piece.
