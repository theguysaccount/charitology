## Why it matters

This is the root condition of the [Diagnosis](/diagnosis/). Close this loop and several others start to close by themselves.

## Where to start

Beneficiary feedback surveys, participatory grantmaking and cash transfers (where recipients choose what to buy) are three existing mechanisms that give the people served some say. The open question is which of them, if any, can be scaled into a signal donors actually follow.
