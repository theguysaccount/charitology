## Why it matters

The [Story Bias](/diagnosis/story-bias/) is one of the best-documented conditions on the list. Telling people about it doesn’t switch it off. The fix has to work with human attention, not against it.

## Where to start

Existing research on identifiable victims and psychic numbing gives the baseline effects to beat. Formats to test include stories of communities rather than individuals, visualizations of scale and stories about sources rather than symptoms.
