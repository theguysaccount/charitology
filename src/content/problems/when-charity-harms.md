## Why it matters

Good intentions are not good outcomes. Some programs have made things worse, and others may have delayed the structural fixes that would have helped more. A science of giving has to study harm as carefully as help.

## Where to start

The [Failure Archive](/record/failure-archive/) collects documented cases. The work is to find the patterns that connect them.
