## Why it matters

Private giving and public spending interact. A gift can unlock government money, replace it or steer it. Each has different consequences for the people served and for democracy.

## Where to start

Economists have studied crowding-in and crowding-out for decades, with mixed results. The open problem is turning that literature into guidance a giver can use.
