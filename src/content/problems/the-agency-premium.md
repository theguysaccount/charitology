## Why it matters

The case for [listening to the people served](/diagnosis/missing-voice/) is strong in principle. A measured premium would make it impossible to ignore, and a measured cost would show where expertise should lead.

## Where to start

Look for interventions that have been delivered both ways, such as community-driven development programs evaluated against centrally planned ones.
