## Why it matters

Millions of people give time, not just money. Some volunteering is invaluable and some costs the organization more to manage than it returns. Nobody can tell a volunteer which is which.

## Where to start

Ask organizations what an hour of each kind of volunteer help is worth to them, and compare that with the volunteer’s wage. See [Time Charitology](/programs/time-charitology/).
