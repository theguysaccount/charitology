## Why it matters

The [Proximity Pull](/diagnosis/proximity-pull/) is partly a moral question and partly an information gap. The information gap can be closed, and then we can see what people choose.

## Where to start

Survey experiments that show donors cost comparisons across distance are cheap to run. The moral side is mapped in the [Disagreement Register](/record/disagreements/).
