## Why it matters

Every choice between two causes is an exchange rate, whether or not anyone writes it down. Writing it down lets people argue about it.

## Where to start

Health economics already compares treatments with quality- and disability-adjusted life years. Wellbeing research proposes life-satisfaction measures. Each scale carries a moral framework inside it. The work is to map which scales each framework accepts and where every scale breaks down. Charitology’s [frameworks data](/tools/public-api/) is a place to begin.
