## Why it matters

Humanity’s Checklist notes that more than $300 billion a year flows into designer goods. If even a share of that status spending became giving, aimed at results, it would change the scale of what charity can do. See [the Prestige Economy](/diagnosis/prestige-economy/).

## Where to start

Humanity’s Checklist proposes charity designer goods, where the coolest thing you can wear shows how much you gave. Testing that proposal, and others like Charitology’s shareable [Wrapped](/tools/charitology-wrapped/) card, is a concrete first experiment.
