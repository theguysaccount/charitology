A field needs a shared vocabulary before it can argue well. The Encyclopedia is that vocabulary at full length: each entry explains one concept, where it came from, what the evidence says and how it connects to the charity system.

Every entry follows [the Charitology Standard](/record/methodology/): plain language, a source for every factual claim, and an honest label where people disagree. Short definitions live in [the Lexicon](/record/lexicon/). Book-length arguments live in [Principles of Charitology](/record/principles/).
