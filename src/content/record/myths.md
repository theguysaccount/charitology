Each entry takes a belief people commonly hold about giving and checks it against the evidence. Verdicts are *false*, *misleading* or *half true*, and each entry says what’s true in the belief before saying what isn’t, then suggests a better question to ask.

## Use this reference base

Search the full text and sources, then filter by editorial verdict, linked system condition or human-review status. Read the full entry for what is true in the belief, where it fails and what to ask instead. Select entries to make a source-linked discussion dossier.

## Coverage and review

Verdicts apply to the belief as worded. They do not judge people who hold it, replace the cited argument or show that a second contributor has reviewed the page.
