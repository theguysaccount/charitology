An *intervention* is a specific thing done to make life better: a bed net, a cash transfer, a law against lead paint. The Database records what each one does, where it sits on the [depth scale](/method/#depth), how strong the evidence is and how long its effects last.

Cost per outcome appears only where a credible source publishes one, and always with its date and caveats. Most source-level work has no such number. That isn’t because it’s worse; it’s because nobody knows how to measure it yet, which is [Open Problem 3](/open-problems/measuring-the-source/). The [Impact Calculator](/tools/impact-calculator/) and the [Compare Tool](/tools/compare-tool/) read straight from this database.
