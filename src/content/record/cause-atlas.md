The Atlas has two layers. The first is the **cause areas** charitology uses in its tools, each placed on five dimensions: time horizon, circle of concern, reach, certainty and typical depth. Those positions are editorial judgments, published so they can be argued with.

The second is the **source map**. Humanity’s Checklist lists eighteen world problems and six sources it argues feed them: story, coordination, vision, attention, status and gatekeepers. The grid below shows which sources the checklist links to which problems. Charitology treats the map as a hypothesis to test, not a finding.
