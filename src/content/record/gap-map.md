Giving is tracked by who gives and by what kind of organization receives. Both views are below, from published estimates. What no dataset tracks is the axis charitology cares about most: **depth**, meaning how much of the money treats symptoms and how much reaches sources.

That missing axis is the gap. Until the [Depth Census](/open-problems/the-depth-census/) measures it, the bars for it stay empty, on purpose.
