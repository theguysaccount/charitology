## Call for papers

The Journal publishes work that moves the field: results on [open problems](/open-problems/), evidence on the [conditions](/diagnosis/), replications, failures and essays that sharpen a debate. Null results and failed replications are especially welcome.

## How review works

Submissions are pull requests. Review happens in public on GitHub, where reviewers sign their comments. A paper is published when two reviewers approve it, and the full review thread stays attached.
