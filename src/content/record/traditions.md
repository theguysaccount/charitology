Each entry describes a tradition on its own terms, using the sources listed there, then asks what its practices suggest about symptoms and sources. The depth reading is Charitology’s interpretation, open to correction by people who practice the tradition.

## Use this reference base

Search across titles, descriptions, practices, interpretive lenses and source names. Filter by the tradition covered or editorial review, then select entries for a reading dossier that keeps their different terms and contexts visible.

## Coverage and review

This is a bounded introduction to the sources represented here, not complete coverage of a religion, culture or every school within it. The depth lens is Charitology’s interpretation, open to correction from people who practice the tradition.
