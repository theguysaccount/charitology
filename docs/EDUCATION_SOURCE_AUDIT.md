## Access and scope limits

- Mason’s public curriculum process is one local example of department, college and university review. Internal CIM and student-organization systems were not accessed. CECiL support does not approve a course or provide class access. Past listed August/September 2026 events are not treated as open opportunities.
- The Philanthropy Lab and Learning by Giving are existing university philanthropy models. No current grant, campus intake, instructor invitation or partnership is promised. Learning by Giving’s refusal of personal grant requests and requests to steer student awards is preserved.
- Campus Compact’s faculty-development and inquiry pages supply public guidance routes. No member access, promotion or institutional credit approval is inferred.
- FCPS separates educational partnerships from procurement. Public partner, volunteer and service-learning summaries do not establish clearance, service-hour eligibility, standards alignment or a research exemption. Linked regulations were not all independently read. Staff must determine the applicable local process.
- McLean’s current club packet is inside Schoology and was not accessed. Its adult contact route stays separate from student contacts. Woodson’s public procedure is explicitly an example from another school, not a substitute for McLean’s packet. Student join codes or contact lists are not retained in the published materials or private outreach notes.
- YPI is a distinct Scottish program. No access, franchise, funding or affiliation transfers to Charitology. Linked forms and downloads were not entered or copied.
- UMD’s public Do Good contact page was read. A separate detailed program page returned HTTP 403 and is not used to infer curriculum or current participation conditions.
- Protected contact addresses were not guessed. Private notes retain only two published university work inquiry addresses actually read on official pages; other destinations remain official contact-page or form routes. Public pages do not publish a staff-contact directory.

The Stripe Directory skill was tried before external organization lookup. The verified official Stripe CLI/plugin rejected the directory query with “Invalid user agent.” Official primary websites were used as the documented fallback. No service was bought or provisioned, and the temporary CLI executable was removed.

## What the sources cannot establish

The proposed 50-minute lesson, eight-session seminar and four-meeting club sequence are Charitology’s untested editorial designs. The Riverbend case and its numbers are fictional. They are not research findings, a tested age recommendation, an accredited course or an independently evaluated educational intervention. The depth scale describes mechanisms rather than merit; the six-source framework stays a hypothesis. No named-charity rating is produced.

The outreach request authorized preparation and public resources. No email, form, application, club registration, subscription, student contact, classroom visit or learner study was carried out. Private drafts and the unsent tracking sheet stay outside Git. Any future exact message needs the owner’s separate send authorization and a current route/source/artifact check.
