## The participant chooses separately

The person must have the current verified account and own-request link. General operational email consent and the new reminder choice are separate. Staff cannot opt in for someone else. Historical email preferences, check-in permission, a booking or a restored file cannot enable reminders.

The own portal reads the current choice before an explicit save. Enabling requires the current confirmed commitment and its exact revisions. Turning the choice off remains available when occurrence approval or ordinary storage capacity no longer permits new work. Revision counters saturate safely rather than preventing that privacy exit. Saving a choice does not schedule or send a message.

Only the latest own-choice command has an exact retry receipt. A later choice replaces that receipt. Reading the current choice and replaying an accepted command are different operations; an old result does not claim to describe the current preference.

## Staff review one audience and message

A current owner or administrator selects 1–20 confirmed participations. Each selection must satisfy current typed permissions, independent source approval, frozen confirmation terms, general email consent, reminder consent, verified recipient binding and the enabled organization delivery policy. One invalid or changed selection refuses the whole command.

The preview shows the exact fixed message and schedule. It expires after ten minutes. Due time is 24 hours before the confirmed occurrence, with the actual named zone, written offset and UTC instant checked. New scheduling requires a future due time and an occurrence within the supported 366-day horizon. Saving a review never creates an email.

The scheduling command rechecks all selections under the organization transaction lock. Its outbox rows, audit and accepted receipt commit together or roll back together. Original commitment uniqueness includes every outbox state. Replaying an accepted staff command returns its original minimal result without adding rows, even after controlled expiry or subsequent source changes; current session and membership authority are still required.

## What the message says

- Subject: **Reminder: review your participation**
- Body: **You asked for a reminder about your participation. Sign in to check the current details. This best-effort reminder does not confirm a place or record attendance.**
- Action: the configured OS origin plus `/participant`, with no query or fragment.

The fixed template includes the established Charitology sender, reply route and postal footer. It contains no occurrence title, location, answers, organization name, roster or receipt. The template is shared with the purpose-limited mail relay and checked for drift. No newsletter subscription, marketing campaign or arbitrary staff-authored email is created.

## Dispatch and uncertain attempts

The existing maintenance dispatcher remains daily at 07:00 UTC, with its shared 20-operation and 45-second bounds. This can miss a reminder window. No exact-time delivery guarantee follows from a due timestamp.

Immediately before a first transport attempt, the worker uses an actual SQL clock and a conditional update under its current lease. First attempts are allowed only at or after due time and strictly before occurrence start. It rechecks current authority, both consents, recipient binding, source, policy and template. A transient configuration refusal can defer work; it does not waive those checks.

An attempted or uncertain message uses the same durable key for status reconciliation. A missing response does not authorize a fresh send. Only a typed relay result proving that no attempt occurred can permit the bounded same-key retry. Provider acceptance, server delivery, inbox placement, reading and attendance remain distinct facts.

Turning off general email or removing the own link clears reminder authority. Verified recipient blocking evidence stops matching unsent work. The implementation does not remove provider suppressions or transfer consent to a changed address.
