# Participation bookings plan

Frozen pre-implementation contract · October 26, 2026 · AI-assisted planning

This is a proposed next Charity OS slice, written before implementation. It does not change the participant portal release or establish that booking software, notification delivery, real participation or independent human approval exists. [The OS plan](CHARITY_OS_PLAN.md), [public intake](PUBLIC_PARTICIPATION.md) and [participant portal](PARTICIPANT_PORTAL.md) retain their separate implementation and evidence records. This planning task makes no runtime, migration, provider, integration, payment or production participant changes.

## Outcome and limits

An authorized staff member can record one confirmed event place or volunteer assignment for one operational contact at one occurrence, after current independent approval, specific participation consent and an atomic capacity check. A full occurrence can retain an explicitly consented waitlist without reserving places. Cancellation releases a place once. Staff deliberately review and promote a waiting request; releasing a place never selects a replacement automatically.

Participants see their own request and participation as separate cards. They can agree to the specific published schedule, choose waitlist and operational-email preferences separately, and cancel their own participation through their verified account. Account ownership still requires the established receipt-plus-verified-email link. Email alone, a public receipt alone and a staff contact's email must not create participant access.

The first slice is deliberately limited to **one person, one place, the full occurrence**. There are no temporary seat holds, automatic offers/expiry, group bookings, payments, sensitive screening, automatic eligibility decisions or automatic waitlist selection. Overnight occurrences are supported through explicit instants and a named time zone. Partial-shift coverage, shared-resource capacity across separate occurrences, recurring schedules, substitutions and actual attendance/check-in require later designs; a field named availability does not implement those features.

## Existing behavior that must be reconciled

The source inspected is [modules.mjs](../services/charity-os/lib/modules.mjs), [record handlers](../services/charity-os/lib/handler.mjs), [intake conversion](../services/charity-os/lib/intake.mjs), [participant access](../services/charity-os/lib/participants.mjs) and [jobs](../services/charity-os/lib/jobs.mjs).

- Events have session, registration and attendance kinds; volunteers have profile, shift, assignment and attendance kinds. Their current module workflows share broad status lists. A generic approved record is an authorization state, not proof of a booking or attendance.
- Current capacity counts active registration/assignment records, including completed records. It checks duplicate contact/occurrence pairs, full-shift volunteer overlaps and reductions below occupied capacity. It does not implement a waitlist, interval coverage or participant booking consent.
- A generic edit clears current approval and returns an approved/active record to review. Counting only that workflow state would silently release a confirmed place after a content edit. Scheduling needs an independent authoritative allocation state.
- Current intake conversion tests a would-be active registration/assignment before creating only a draft. A full occurrence therefore blocks even preparation of a waitlist candidate. Future conversion must distinguish a nonallocating candidate from an allocating confirmation; do not simply disable capacity validation on the actual booking path.
- Current participant claim/collection consent authorizes access and operational answer review. It is not specific agreement to attend an occurrence. Existing request withdrawal, answer erasure and permanent unlink do not silently cancel a separate booking.
- The current mail relay permits only login, invitation and reminder purposes, and its reminder URL points to the staff app. Neither that purpose nor its recipient checks authorizes participant booking email. The existing dispatcher must not accidentally send new event rows through a generic fallthrough.

## Four independent facts

| Fact | What it means | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request and collection consent | The organization may review the submitted operational answers for the displayed purpose. | Attendance intent, identity beyond the existing claim proof, approval, a place or newsletter consent. |
| Staff authorization | A currently permitted staff actor acts on an independently approved saved candidate and approved occurrence revision. | Capacity was available earlier, the participant agreed to changed terms or that a real human performed a review during a test. |
| Scheduling fulfillment | The canonical ledger records requested, waitlisted, confirmed or cancelled, with a revision and frozen allocation provenance. | Attendance, screening clearance, payment, service eligibility or improved charitable outcomes. |
| Notification outcome | A separate outbox event is paused, queued, accepted by a provider or later reported delivered. | A reserved place from an email alone, inbox/read proof from API acceptance or permission to bypass another purpose's preferences. |

Approval snapshots remain server-derived and independently bound to saved revisions. A confirmed allocation retains the authorization and schedule it relied on even if a later working draft returns to review. Later edits must not manufacture approval, silently change the promised time or release its capacity.

## Canonical allocation and legacy adoption

Use one strict-tenant fulfillment ledger, proposed as `os_participations`, plus target-management metadata, proposed as `os_participation_targets`. Both enable and force RLS under the constrained runtime role. Do not create a second independently maintained capacity counter or count managed generic records again.

| Proposed entity | Minimum responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Managed target | Organization/target identity, revision, managed/paused/closed mode, adoption provenance, independently approved source revision, deliberate public schedule snapshot and hash. The original session/shift remains the typed source. |
| Participation | Organization/id/revision; target and typed booking-record references; contact and optional original intake reference; source kind staff/intake/legacy; fulfillment state; candidate approval provenance; approved schedule hash/revision; participation/waitlist consent versions and times; waitlist sequence; confirmation/cancellation times; frozen allocation schedule; opaque actor IDs. |
| Notification outbox | Unique causal event identity, participation/revision/template/channel, recipient account/link reference, applicable preference version, safe payload hash and paused/delivery state. No raw receipts or answer payloads. |

Use tenant-composite foreign keys. A typed registration/assignment maps to at most one ledger entry. One noncancelled contact/target participation prevents duplicate allocation or waitlist entries. An intake/target association also cannot be converted twice. Cancelled entries are historical: reapplication is an explicit new request, not automatic revival of the old authority.

Before enabling a target, an administrator previews **all** existing active generic registrations/assignments for that occurrence and the hash/revisions of that set. This set also includes prior confirmed recovered occupancy even when recovery reset its generic source to draft. Under the organization lock, adoption rechecks the exact set and creates one ledger representation per occupant, deduplicating only an exact shared source identity. Known completed history remains historical occupancy for that same occurrence; it is not a free seat to resell. Preserve null consent/approval when legacy provenance is unknown. Label it recorded legacy/recovered occupancy, not newly consented participation or independently reviewed eligibility. Adoption creates no participant link or notification.

Any duplicate, broken reference, over-capacity result or conflicting volunteer overlap stops adoption as a whole with a private reconciliation report. Nothing is silently dropped or partially imported. Until adoption succeeds, the old target retains its legacy behavior and the new booking commands are unavailable. Once managed, its ledger is the sole allocation source and every generic activation/cancellation/import path must go through the shared gateway.

## Participation consent and staff journeys

**Participant request.** Through the existing active own-request link, show the independently approved occurrence's title, start/end instants, named zone, participation purpose and deliberately published instructions. Internal joining URLs, venue details, staff notes or contact identifiers must not be copied blindly into a public snapshot. The participant makes three separate, initially unchecked choices: specific participation request/consent, willingness to remain on a waitlist, and operational email updates. Collection consent, account-link consent and newsletter consent remain distinct.

The frozen public terms derive only from the independently approved target: title, occurrence kind, start/end instants, named time zone and new typed `participationPurpose` (maximum 600 characters), `participantInstructions` (600), `organizerEmail` (254) and `organizerUrl` (HTTPS, 500). At least one organizer email or URL is required. These deliberately published fields are the organizer's supplied contact route, not a claim that support was independently tested. Do not copy `joiningUrl` or arbitrary private fields.

Consent binds **both the exact approved target source revision and the canonical public-terms hash/version**. Even a nonmaterial source revision requires fresh consent in this first slice; there is no hash-equivalence exception. Keep the managed-target configuration revision distinct from the approved typed source revision, and check both where they govern a command. Capacity is validated separately but cannot bypass the current approval/revision checks. Do not infer new consent from login, a viewed page, an old form checkbox or a Contact preference.

**Staff preparation.** An authorized owner/admin or appropriately scoped events/volunteer writer associates the reviewed intake with its existing typed registration/assignment and explicitly chosen contact. No email-only merge or account grant occurs. A full occurrence may still have a nonallocating requested candidate. Required operational prerequisites stay unknown until an authorized staff decision is recorded. A safeguarding reference is not an automated screening result, and restricted reasons never appear to participants.

**Staff confirmation.** Another authorized reviewer approves the candidate's saved revision under the existing independence policy. The candidate must be in its approved workflow state. Approval alone consumes no place. Staff then choose Confirm participation, with expected candidate, occurrence, participation and consent revisions. The server rechecks current module-write membership/program scope, independent approval, specific consent, target openness, duplicate and capacity/overlap rules under one lock. New confirmation requires a future occurrence; it cannot manufacture a full-occurrence commitment after its start. Success reserves exactly one place, advances the generic record to the appropriate scheduled/assigned projection and stores frozen provenance atomically. The UI says Confirmation recorded; it does not claim delivery or attendance. Only administrators may adopt legacy targets; ordinary fulfillment commands require the relevant module-write permission and program scope.

**Full occurrence and waitlist.** Staff choose Waitlist only after explicit waitlist consent and applicable authorization. This reserves zero places. Order entries by a server-assigned sequence when they become valid waiting candidates. Show staff the earliest eligible entry as a suggestion; selecting another requires a bounded operational reason, not sensitive scoring. No algorithm decides who is deserving. Participants see Waitlisted — no place is reserved, without other names, roster counts, rank, private ineligibility reasons or a promised promotion date.

**A place becomes available.** Cancellation prompts staff to review the waitlist. Promotion is a separate explicit confirmation command. Recheck current source/target approval, consent, sequence, contact duplicates, overlap and capacity; a stale suggestion cannot reserve a place. No release, import, cron tick or email webhook automatically selects or confirms another person.

**Offline participation.** Staff may record a minimal consent provenance reference, method and time for an offline participant, without requiring an email. That is a staff-recorded assertion, not verified mailbox identity or automatically verified consent. It creates no account or portal access. A declared organizer cancellation route remains necessary for people without an account and for permanently unlinked participants; publish only approved operational contact information.

## State and action rules

| Action | Required previous state | Result and capacity effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Associate candidate | No existing noncancelled entry for that contact/target | Requested; zero places. Never equate intake conversion or independent approval with confirmation. |
| Waitlist | Requested, with current explicit waitlist consent | Waitlisted; zero places; stable server order. |
| Confirm | Requested with current approvals and specific consent | Confirmed; one place after an atomic final check. |
| Promote | Waitlisted with current approvals and consent | Confirmed; one place after the same final check plus explicit staff action/queue reason. |
| Cancel participation | Requested, waitlisted or confirmed | Cancelled once; releases one place only if it was confirmed. No replacement promotion. Repeat same intent returns the saved outcome. |
| Record attendance/completion | Separate permitted operational action | Preserve the occurrence's historical allocation. A booking is not attendance, and completing a record does not create a new free place for that same occurrence. |

There are no temporary holds or automatic offer expirations in this slice. Availability is computed at commit, not promised by a displayed count. A second person competing for the final place receives a conflict and can explicitly choose a waitlist; the server does not silently move them to a different state.

## Capacity, concurrency and cross-module invariants

- Acquire the existing organization advisory lock first, then lock target, participation and referenced candidate/intake rows in a fixed order. Re-read all permissions and source revisions inside that transaction. Participant operations additionally require an active own link; a known contact ID or receipt does not substitute.
- Confirmed ledger entries alone consume one place each on managed targets. Requested/waitlisted/cancelled entries consume zero. Unknown capacity is not unlimited; zero is zero. New confirmation requires a known valid capacity and enough remaining places. Display separate recorded legacy occupancy when its provenance remains unknown.
- Enforce duplicate contact/target and intake/target constraints in the database as well as application validation. Two final-seat transactions cannot both commit. Staff UI, generic module actions, intake conversion, integration imports, bulk operations and participant cancellation all use the same command path.
- Put the event/volunteer guard in the central record save path, with a server-internal helper flag for legitimate ledger mutations; never trust an API/body flag. Add delete/restore guards as well. Avoid scattered API-only validation overlays that a second caller can bypass.
- Check volunteer overlaps using confirmed **frozen** allocation instants and half-open intervals `[start,end)`: adjacent shifts are allowed, overlap is not. Validate explicit offsets and the named zone, including overnight and daylight-saving transitions. Do not silently extend this rule to event conflicts without an agreed policy. Unknown or invalid intervals cannot become confirmed assignments.
- Managed and unmanaged shifts must check each other: an unmanaged assignment or shift edit cannot collide with a managed frozen allocation just because its generic record is back in review. A managed confirmation must also include valid unmanaged legacy occupants. A cross-program conflict can be reported generically without revealing another program's private roster or schedule.
- A working-content edit returning a generic record to review does not release or move its confirmed ledger reservation. Show the review state beside fulfillment. Guard allocation fields such as target, contact, program and schedule from ordinary PATCH/import changes; amendment/cancel commands must own them.
- Block ordinary identity/time/program changes and parent delete/cancel until all noncancelled participations are resolved. This includes allocation contact/target reassignment. A content review cannot free a confirmed place. The first slice requires explicit cancellation and a newly approved replan with fresh consent before moving to a changed occurrence. Do not include a general live-rescheduling engine in this base release. Historical completed occupancy remains bound to its original occurrence.
- Target capacity cannot drop below ledger occupancy. A capacity change does not release existing allocations and still requires current independent source approval; its changed target revision also requires fresh consent before a subsequent confirmation/promotion. Any later rescheduling feature must validate all affected frozen volunteer intervals and make replacement atomic; two unrelated requests are not a safe reassignment implementation.
- Cancelling/deleting the parent is blocked until staff explicitly cancels its nonterminal participation entries through the gateway. Never leave an effective confirmed reservation on a cancelled parent. A later bulk-cancel command needs preflight limits and an all-or-nothing outcome; it is not required for the first individual-booking slice and must not silently truncate work.
- Reuse typed record and workspace budgets, expected revisions, append-only provenance and validated backups. New retained caps are **500 managed targets / 2 MiB**, **2,000 participations / 8 MiB including cancelled and legacy rows**, and **8,000 paused outbox rows / 8 MiB**, with at most **2 KiB per outbox payload**. Include all three entities in the existing 60 MiB logical workspace budget and preserve the existing emergency reserve. Measure actual retained UTF-8/Postgres JSON representation rather than trusting client lengths. New ordinary allocations fail atomically if history/outbox capacity cannot be retained. Cancellation and preference withdrawal remain available at full ordinary budget, using minimal emergency-safe audit and an explicit notification-not-queued result if an outbox event cannot be retained.

## Request privacy actions and participant visibility

Keep the **Request** card and **Participation** card separate. The first reports pending/reviewed/converted/withdrawn/answers-erased. The second reports needs participation consent/awaiting staff decision/waitlisted/confirmed/cancelled plus its accepted schedule and permitted actions. A request marked converted is not automatically confirmed.

Authenticated Cancel participation checks the own link, booking identity and expected booking revision, releases its place once and returns the saved result. It does not cancel another request, delete a contact or promote the next person. Public receipt withdrawal keeps its earlier request-only authority and does not gain the ability to cancel a confirmed place. All affected pages must explain the distinction clearly. Staff can cancel through the authorized offline route with provenance.

Cancellation requires current proper access and scope, not fresh allocation approval or attendance consent. A later content edit clearing approval must never strand a participant or prevent safe release. Fresh candidate/target approval and specific consent gate new allocation and promotion; they do not make a properly authorized cancellation depend on another reviewer.

Request answer erasure and permanent account unlink do not silently cancel an independent participation record. Explain the retained minimal allocation/history and separately created contact/backup boundaries before either action. Before unlinking an active place, warn that this account cannot re-link to regain self-service cancellation and show the approved organizer contact route. Unlink removes access and future account-addressed unsent notifications; it does not grant a new email-only recovery route.

Closing or expiring an intake publication stops new collection, not the occurrence or existing confirmations. Existing own booking visibility and cancellation remain available. A waitlist recorded before collection closure may be promoted only if its separate participation consent, target/candidate approvals and own request association remain valid. A subsequently **withdrawn intake blocks requested/waitlisted confirmation or promotion until fresh specific participation affirmation after that withdrawal**; existing confirmations remain until explicit booking cancellation. Both public receipt cancellation and authenticated request withdrawal must persist a durable server `cancelled_at` intake timestamp. Compare fresh consent against that authoritative withdrawal, not a client-supplied time. Erasing raw answers alone is not withdrawal of separately recorded participation consent; its UI must say so.

Participant responses expose only their own booking ID/state/revision, approved public schedule/purpose/instructions, relevant consent/preferences and safe cancellation information. No staff actors/reasons, internal record/contact IDs, roster, queue rank, other accounts, private screening status or full audit history. Any optional ICS export is explicit and contains only that approved schedule, a stable event UID and clean portal URL; no receipt, email, roster or private notes.

## Paused notification outbox

Persist notification intent in a dedicated outbox in the same transaction as the corresponding booking change. A uniqueness constraint such as organization/participation/event revision/purpose/channel prevents duplicate intent. Payloads hold causal IDs, template/version and an immutable safe payload hash, not raw intake answers, staff notes, receipts, arbitrary URLs or copied sensitive data. Do not insert a new unrecognized job into the existing dispatcher and hope its reminder branch is safe.

**First slice: paused events only.** No provider call or actual notification is implemented by this plan. The portal is the canonical status view and must say Email updates are not enabled or Updates off as appropriate. A stored preference or paused event does not mean queued for delivery, sent or delivered. No new account, paid provider, secret or campaign is provisioned.

The **next bounded implementation slice after base bookings** must complete delivery through the existing purpose-limited relay, rather than provision another provider or leave notifications permanently deferred. It must explicitly add an operational participation purpose and clean `/participant` path to the relay's allowlist, extend its validation/templates/tests and complete the controlled delivery checks below. Immediately before dispatch it must recheck the active own link, current verified recipient binding, current per-participation email consent, event relevance and applicable suppression. Login/invitation/reminder permissions cannot be borrowed, and newsletter Contacts/Topics/Segments/suppression stay untouched. Offline people receive no invented address or automatic email.

Use a stable provider idempotency identity and immutable payload for one causal event. Persist attempts, leases, next-at times and bounded safe error codes. A timeout with ambiguous acceptance becomes Unknown for reconciliation, not an automatic new-key send after provider idempotency expires. Provider acceptance, signed/deduplicated delivery feedback and inbox receipt are distinct evidence; none proves the participant read or understood the change. A mail failure must not roll back or duplicate a committed booking.

Opt-out/unlink cancels eligible unsent events without cancelling the place. Later schedule/cancellation events supersede stale unsent notifications. Preference withdrawal and cancellation remain safe at quota limits. A paused backlog must never flush automatically when a future delivery flag is enabled: stale events stay paused/cancelled until relevance and consent are explicitly reconciled. Daily dispatch is not immediate delivery; without proven actual transport, no reminder or change-notification reliability is promised. A controlled owned-recipient HTML/plain-text/privacy/idempotence exercise is required before enabling this purpose. The base release must disable its email preference control with a clear unavailable state, or label it explicitly Preference only — no messages sent in this release.

## Proposed command surface

These are proposed contracts for review, not implemented endpoints. Staff routes start with `/api/orgs/:orgId/participation-targets/:targetId`; current membership/module/program scope and CSRF apply. Participant routes remain under the existing own request proof.

| Method/path | Planned input and result |
| --- | --- |
| `GET staff target` | Current target revision, adoption/reconciliation status, capacity and private authorized roster summary. No adoption side effects. |
| `POST staff target/adopt` | `{targetRevision,expectedLegacyHash}`; rechecks the whole preview and adopts exact legacy occupancy or rejects it all. |
| `POST staff target/participations` | Associate an existing candidate/intake with expected revisions and an explicitly chosen contact; create Requested with no allocation. |
| `GET staff target/participations?cursor=…` | Scoped ledger pages of 20, with staff-only provenance and state filters. |
| `POST staff target/participations/:id/waitlist`, `/confirm`, `/promote`, `/cancel` | Expected participation/target/candidate/consent revisions, bounded reason and stable idempotency key. All commands use the shared allocation gateway. |
| `GET participant own request` | Existing allowlisted Request plus a distinct own participation summary, without staff/contact/roster fields. |
| `POST participant own request/participations/:id/consent` | Expected participation/intake and displayed schedule revisions, explicit participation consent, separately chosen waitlist/email preferences. No arbitrary target/contact selection. |
| `POST participant own request/participations/:id/cancel` | Expected booking revision and explicit cancellation; return the saved cancelled outcome. No auto-promotion or new grants. |

Corrections to booking-defining details require a new approved proposal and consent, not arbitrary participant PATCH. Cancellation is terminal for this first slice; reapplication requires a new typed candidate. Staff target cancellation and notification-preference opt-out use similarly revisioned, scoped commands. A general amendment/rescheduling command is later work; the base policy blocks identity/time/program edits until explicit cancel/replan and prevents capacity reduction below occupancy.

Use a bounded **latest-command** idempotency contract. Clients generate a random key of 16–100 characters; the server stores hashes of actor/action/key and canonical payload, not the raw key. After fresh authorization, an exact retry of the latest command returns its saved state with no new writes, allocation, audit or outbox event. Reusing that same intent with a changed payload returns 409. After an intervening mutation, an older intent's expected revision returns 409; recover with GET rather than automatically replaying it. Do not promise an unlimited historical command registry, automatic replay or backlog flush.

## Recovery without false availability

Export the target, participation, public schedule and paused outbox provenance within the existing backup checksum/size envelope. Restore validates these through one helper and marks them recovered/inactive. It restores no active participant access, trusted approval/consent, automatic reservation or pending sender. Imported roles, old assertions of confirmation and source identities do not become authority.

Inactive cannot mean apparently empty. A recovered target with prior confirmed occupancy is **recovery-blocked** from new allocation until an administrator reconciles it. The adoption preview must include that recovered claimed occupancy even when the generic registration/assignment now has draft status. Fresh independently approved target source and the exact reconciliation hash are required before enabling it; the operator must resolve actual operating commitments explicitly. Retain recovered legacy/unknown provenance, and never silently discard a claim to make capacity look available. No recovered outbox backlog is eligible for automatic sending.

## Frozen implementation boundary

The coordinator has frozen one allocation ledger, administrator-only exact legacy adoption, one full-occurrence place, no holds, manual staff confirmation/promotion, current module/program authorization, approved candidate/target sources, separate specific consent, exact source-revision plus public-terms hash matching, the stated quotas/latest-command replay rules, withdrawal chronology and a paused outbox. Identity/time/program changes and parent delete/cancel wait for explicit cancellation of all noncancelled participations; capacity cannot drop below occupancy. Central save/delete/restore guards protect managed and unmanaged paths. Recovery is inactive provenance with no reservations, participant grants or sends, and prior confirmed occupancy recovery-blocks the target until deliberate reconciliation. No bulk cancellation or general rescheduling engine belongs in the base slice. The following slice completes delivery through the existing relay with its separately proven consent/transport contract.

The plan does not authorize automatic sensitive screening, payment handling, a real event, new integrations, real-person invitations or notification delivery. Staff authorization and independent approval are software controls; no test or status label establishes that an independent human actually reviewed a real participant or that a scheduled activity happened.
