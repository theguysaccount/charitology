# Charity networks source audit

Prepared October 28, 2026 UTC. All 18 entries remain `review: draft`. This is an AI-assisted editorial source audit, not independent human review, a network recommendation or a statement of partnership. Sources are the organizations’ own descriptions; the project has not evaluated their outcomes.

## Reading scope

The committed data preserves **58 source records, each with its actual reading time, support statement and access status**. Fifty-four are full-page readings and four explicitly record limited access. Every listing has at least two full official-source readings. There are six founder entries, six leadership/professional entries and six sector/infrastructure entries. Listings are alphabetical within each guide; their numbers are navigation, not scores.

Three separate research passes covered the categories, followed by cross-checks of the other categories. Those checks are AI assistance and do not satisfy the project’s independent human review requirements. Current source summaries and engagement links are in `src/data/charity-networks.mjs`; the public guides expose source notes, limits and dates. No application, membership, account, purchase, message or contact import was made.

## Access limits and corrections

- Ambitious Impact’s officially linked Tally expression-of-interest form was read only through its public introduction. No later steps were entered. The substantive program claims come from its three official program pages.
- CSAE’s earlier contact page redirected to sign-in during cross-checking. It remains a limited-access historical source note; the public action now opens the readable membership page, not that login route. CSAE serves association professionals and is not restricted to charities.
- SAIF’s dated FAQ was limited; the main site and membership page support the narrower description. It is an individual fundraising-profession body, not a general chief-executive association. No member-list or fundraising service is offered by the listing.
- TechSoup’s About page returned an access refusal. Three other read official pages support technology services and participation. Country and product qualifications still apply; directory inclusion supplies no entitlement.
- ACEVO mentoring and advice are described as membership-tier dependent. Charity Leadership Scotland’s current brand and published audience are retained. SILA and Sun Ray are selective leadership programs, not unrestricted peer memberships; one SILA criteria page was read by the original pass but timed out during its peer recheck.
- CIVICUS membership and contact actions use the working `www.civicus.org` host, re-read by the coordinator. Older membership policy material is identified by its source, without copying dated processing promises or membership totals.
- D-Prize is described around distribution of existing poverty interventions, not general software or open-ended research funding. School for Social Entrepreneurs includes social enterprises and community interest companies; it is not a charity-only network.

## Selected coverage and exclusions

This is an initial set, not global coverage or a list of the best organizations. Membership conditions, programs, deadlines and fees may change; the guides do not claim applications are currently open or promise selection or responses. No private member lists, personal contact profiles or guessed email addresses are published. A network may provide support at the structural level of the charity system; that description does not grade the work done by its members.

Ashoka, the Tampa Nonprofit Leadership Center and ICVA were not added because current engagement or program pages could not be sufficiently rechecked. Older accessible documents were not treated as proof of current access. Water For People was separately excluded from the private software outreach proposal because its official contact page rejects unsolicited technology/product inquiries. Directory inclusion and outreach selection are separate decisions.

## Reproduction and review

The build imports `charity-network-guides.mjs`, which refuses duplicate IDs, unsupported categories, non-draft entries, missing prose, unsafe URLs, missing reading records and fewer than two full readings per listing. The actual source text was read externally and is not vendored or relicensed here. This validation cannot establish that a source is correct, that conditions remain unchanged or that a human reviewed it.

Private research notes preserve the original reading records and cross-check findings. They are excluded from the Git release. Human review and future corrections should refer to the exact committed data version and the organization’s current source page.
