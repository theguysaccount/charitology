# Fundraising hub source audit

Checked October 28, 2026 UTC. This is an editorial source and implementation review, not a fundraising effectiveness study. The source pages below were actually opened during this review unless an access limitation says otherwise. No donor retention percentage or universal method ranking is imported into the hub.

The ten Charitology playbooks and four follow-up reminders are our untested editorial proposals. Primary-source links explain their context; a link does not make each suggested step a tested finding. Money raised is an input to charitable work, not evidence of outcomes or causal impact.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Permitted use | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Benchmark | Compare a clearly defined measure with a described participating cohort. | Observational context does not identify a universally effective channel or explain causation. |
| Provider experiment | Examine the stated audience, comparison, dates, outcome and validity information. | A provider's reported experiment is context-specific; its result is not an independent replication or a guarantee for another charity. |
| Professional or program guidance | Understand recommended conduct, training, program conditions or operational practices. | Guidance and eligibility rules are not comparative effectiveness evidence. Jurisdiction and current conditions matter. |
| Peer discussion | Find questions and experiences worth investigating. | Participants are self-selected; identities, facts and results are not independently established by the thread. |
| Charitology editorial proposal | Prepare a bounded local test and record costs, workload, follow-up and uncertainty. | The playbooks, arithmetic planner and illustrative timing have not demonstrated better fundraising results. |

## Primary resources read

### M+R Benchmarks

Read the [2026 study and methodology](https://mrbenchmarks.com/), plus its [email](https://mrbenchmarks.com/email-messaging/), [advertising](https://mrbenchmarks.com/advertising/), [fundraising](https://mrbenchmarks.com/fundraising/), [direct-mail](https://mrbenchmarks.com/direct-mail/) and [glossary](https://mrbenchmarks.com/glossary/) pages used in the source capsules. The methodology describes participant data, sector and size groupings, medians and incomplete metric coverage. It warns against directly comparing annual editions because the participant pool and some calculations change. The advertising page distinguishes spending relative to revenue from effectiveness or return. The fundraising page distinguishes online one-time donor retention from monthly-giver survival. Use these as cohort context with explicit denominators, rather than targets every charity should reach or evidence that a channel caused better results. The audit does not import a headline fundraising or retention number.

### Fundraising Effectiveness Project

Read the [official AFP project page](https://afpglobal.org/FundraisingEffectivenessProject) and the [FEP report index](https://fepreports.org/) used in the source capsule. They identify the AFP Foundation for Philanthropy/GivingTuesday collaboration, reports and participating data sources. Also read GivingTuesday's [2021 methodology update](https://www.givingtuesday.org/blog/fundraising-effectiveness-project-methodology-update/), which explains late data and revisions to historical top-line estimates. The older update explains a methodological concern; it is not asserted to be the complete current methodology. No individual current quarterly report is reviewed by this audit. No retention percentage is copied into the hub.

### NextAfter

Read the provider's [research library](https://www.nextafter.com/research/) and [experiment index](https://www.nextafter.com/all-experiments/). These contain experiments, research studies and provider services; the index includes differing validity labels. Read one concrete [video-versus-static advertising experiment](https://www.nextafter.com/experiments/how-video-ads-affect-email-acquisition-rate-for-a-free-course/) as a check on the label: it describes a particular organization's split audience, dates and email acquisition outcome, with a negative treatment result. That is not a donor revenue experiment or proof that video is generally worse. The public hub should describe a provider research resource, while its playbooks propose local tests without borrowing advertised effect sizes or presenting them as independent evidence.

### AFP Donor Bill of Rights

Read the [original AFP page](https://afpglobal.org/donor-bill-rights). It presents professional principles for information, acknowledgment, confidentiality and donor relationships. These support the hub's attention to truthful communication and stewardship as normative guidance, not as a tested revenue intervention. AFP states that reproducing the document requires permission; the hub links to the original and uses its own brief editorial descriptions rather than reproducing the text.

### Candid Learning — indexed description only

Actual opens of [Candid Learning](https://learning.candid.org/), [Introduction to Fundraising Planning](https://learning.candid.org/introduction-to-fundraising-planning?reg=1) and [Introduction to Individual Giving](https://learning.candid.org/introduction-to-individual-giving/387180) returned **403 Forbidden**. The official indexed descriptions of those two courses were read. They describe fundraising planning, a case for support, diversified funding, an action plan and an introductory individual-giving program. That supports only cautiously labeled learning-resource links. The recordings, linked handouts, class curricula, current places, pricing and access conditions were not reviewed. Do not label the courses as completed, evaluated or proven effective on this basis.

### Google Ad Grants

Read the [official program FAQ](https://www.google.com/grants/faq/). It describes an in-kind search advertising program with nonprofit eligibility, website and ongoing policy requirements. This is provider program guidance, not evidence that advertising will produce net income. Ad credit is not received cash, and preparation, website work, measurement and staff time still belong in a local plan. The audit makes no eligibility decision and initiates no enrollment, account, spending or provider connection.

### NTEN

Read the [official NTEN homepage](https://www.nten.org/) and [Connect page](https://www.nten.org/connect). They identify nonprofit technology education, online groups and local clubs; the Connect page describes a free account requirement for its community platform. This supports a professional learning/community link. It does not establish the effectiveness of a fundraising method, make each member's advice authoritative or verify individual discussion claims.

### Rogare

Read the [publications index](https://www.rogare.net/publications). It organizes research reports, white papers and discussion papers about fundraising ethics, relationships, stakeholder engagement and professional practice. The linked reports were not individually reviewed. The source capsule appropriately identifies an index for further reading, rather than evidence that the ten proposed workflows were tested or a ranking of methods.

### National Council of Nonprofits

Read [Gift Acknowledgments](https://www.councilofnonprofits.org/running-nonprofit/fundraising-and-resource-development/gift-acknowledgments-saying-thank-you-donors) and [Corporate Sponsorship](https://www.councilofnonprofits.org/running-nonprofit/fundraising-and-resource-development/corporate-sponsorship). The first discusses acknowledgment timing, recognition preferences and formal gift documentation; the second discusses readiness, written expectations and tax/private-benefit questions. The capsules use these as practice guidance. This audit does not adopt their tax descriptions as legal advice, reproduce receipt language, classify an arrangement or generate formal gift documentation.

### Peer forum context

Read the [public r/nonprofit community page](https://www.reddit.com/r/nonprofit/), including its visible discussion descriptions and the highlighted prohibition on market research/solicitation. It is a place to read practitioner questions, not a recruited research sample, a source of measured retention results or an invitation to solicit users. No post was made and no participant was contacted. Public hub summaries should not disclose or reproduce donor information, private figures or individual contributors' stories.

### Additional jurisdiction-specific guidance

Read the [UK Fundraising Regulator's Code of Fundraising Practice landing page](https://www.fundraisingregulator.org.uk/code). It identifies the code's UK scope and the version effective in November 2025. If linked, label it UK professional/regulatory guidance; do not apply it as a universal rule or claim to have reviewed every underlying provision. It is not comparative fundraising research.
