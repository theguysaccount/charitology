# Concept and charter evidence audit

Date: October 25, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). AI-assisted editorial audit; this is not the independent human review required by `HANDOFF.md` and `STYLE.md`.

All **109 files** in the seven folders below were read for claim scope, evidence labels, style, proposed versus live status and the rule against ratings of named charities. **No file was marked reviewed.** All 109 omit an explicit `review` field and therefore remain `draft` under the schema default.

Primary materials were checked at passage level where indicated below. A source that loaded, a plausible claim, or an unchanged paragraph is not a completed source review. Sources not matched to a checked item remain explicitly outstanding. The human reviewer must check every claim, including claims whose source was only partly consulted.

## Material corrections

- DAF assets grew 27.5%, from $255.99 billion to $326.45 billion, rather than 30% from $250 billion. The 25.3% payout rate uses prior-year assets; it is not the share of every account spent in a year.
- CEP’s 11% figure is conditional on the 58% of surveyed foundations providing some multiyear general support. It is not 11% of all funders.
- The IRS exempt-organization count is not a count of 501(c)(3) charities. The private-foundation distribution rule is not a flat 5% of every asset paid as grants.
- Story Bias is now debated, reflecting the hypothetical-donation replication. Silence About Failure is now a hypothesis because an example of disclosure does not establish how often silence occurs or what it does to funding.
- Unsupported majority claims, donor/founder motives, universal absence claims and claims that a deeper intervention necessarily achieves more were removed or narrowed. Domain availability is not evidence that philanthropy lacked a field.
- The deworming disagreement now distinguishes pooled follow-up periods from an exact 20-year outcome. Both quoted estimates meet p<0.10, not p<0.05.
- Fund for Shared Insight is described as concluded and archived. Program issue instructions use the generic `program` label. The unfunded bounty program no longer invites readers to send money.
- No named-charity rating was created. The poem remains intact with a short editorial context note. Draft statuses and program readiness statuses were preserved.
