# Record source audit

Date: October 25, 2026. This pass used AI assistance. It is a source and wording audit, not the second-contributor review required by `STYLE.md`. No entry was marked `reviewed`.

## Scope

Read every file in `src/content/interventions/` (15), `cases/` (13), `myths/` (8), `traditions/` (8), `charities/` (16) and `forecasts/` (5), plus `src/data/figures.json`: 65 entries and one shared data file. Checked selected high-risk claims against the sources below. Reading an entry does not mean every source or every claim in it was independently checked.

## Corrections made

- Replaced the bed-net calculator's range for all GiveWell top charities with GiveWell's explicitly dated range for net campaigns. Kept its modeled status and location limits.
- Replaced the older vitamin A calculator range with the later, explicitly dated estimate for the August and September 2024 grants. The entry describes those grants rather than predicting a new donation's result.
- Changed the Zambia Teaching at the Right Level cost to the point estimate for broad foundational math. The previous low and high values came from different tests, not an uncertainty interval. Kept the narrower targeted-skill result in the note.
- Corrected the deworming follow-up description after the parallel source audit checked the primary PNAS paper: the consumption and hourly-earnings estimates pool different follow-up rounds, rather than both representing the 20-year round alone.
- Corrected the Scared Straight documentary's broadcast year in the case and myth, using OJJDP's account.
- Narrowed the smallpox case and polio forecast's claim about eradication ending spending. Ending natural transmission does not end preparedness, laboratory safeguards or every other polio-related task. Removed the smallpox patient's identifying details in line with the content standard.
- Corrected the blanket exclusion of immediate family from zakat. Eligible siblings may receive it, and restrictions differ by school. Qualified broad statements about dāna across three religions and labeled the tzedakah connection to Charitology's depth scale as interpretation.
- Replaced secondary headline giving sources with Giving USA's own press release and added the five previously missing recipient subsectors. Explained why the nine rows do not exhaust all uses of giving or sum to the headline total.
- Corrected the DAF figures and forecast background to the primary report's Table 3. Distinguished fiscal years, estimates and the aggregate payout calculation. The forecast now specifies the first FY 2026 estimate and excludes later revisions.

## What remains open

- Human review remains required for every draft. This audit did not change that gate.
- The full trial literature for community health workers, food banks, Housing First, deworming, vaccination incentives, vitamin A, animal campaigns and ORT was not reread. Evidence grades remain draft judgments.
- The full source sets for the historical cases were not checked. In particular, Haiti's disputed claims still require reading the investigation and the Red Cross response together; ALS attribution limits, PlayPumps field reports, and the Rosenwald and hookworm studies need full-paper review.
- Hindu, Jain, Christian, Jewish, ubuntu and mutual-aid coverage still needs specialist review of the cited texts and the range of interpretations. Reading selected sources does not establish that a tradition has one settled view.
- Several source requests were blocked or incomplete: the bed-net Cochrane article returned a browser check; FEMA, D.A.R.E.'s history and NobelPrize.org's 2006 pages returned access errors; the first browser request for the DAF PDF failed, after which the PDF was retrieved and checked locally. A failed fetch is not verification.
- Cost figures are dated models from particular programs and settings. This pass did not establish a universal cost for any intervention or show what the next donation will achieve.
- Forecast outcomes remain open, with no probabilities supplied. Resolution sources and deadlines are definitions, not evidence that a question will resolve either way. Early public resolution must be handled before accepting later forecasts.
