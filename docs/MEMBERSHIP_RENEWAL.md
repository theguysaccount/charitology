# Membership renewal

In [My requests](https://os.charitology.org/participant), open a linked contact request that staff have converted to one Contact. Choose **Your membership renewal**, then **Ask staff to review my membership**. This starts the private review for that request.

In the shared workspace, an authorized owner or administrator opens the converted contact request in **Public forms and requests** and chooses **Review membership renewal**. Select the exact Memberships member record and approved renewal for the same Contact. Review the proposed binding before saving. The renewal record needs independent approval and a contiguous successor term with **Not required** as the dues status.

After the binding, the member reviews the dates and requests renewal in **My requests**. Staff open the same review and confirm it. The decision extends current coverage from its existing start date. A saved dues reference still shows payment as unverified. If a save response is interrupted, choose **Retry the same membership change** or reopen the saved review.

Directory choice is separate. Members can record or withdraw their own preference from the renewal panel. The workspace keeps this as a private choice. Ordinary membership records also support staff handling for members who use another channel.
