# Renew a membership in person

Use this workflow when a member wants help renewing in person. Agree how your organization will check identity and accept a withdrawal before using the workflow. An owner or administrator with access to the whole workspace opens the member's saved Memberships record and chooses **Renew in person**. Work from the member's current Contact and an independently approved next term. The term must begin the day after current coverage ends and require no payment.

Read the current and proposed dates to the member and explain how to withdraw a pending choice. Record their affirmative choice only while they are present. The panel asks for an acknowledgment and does not take identity documents, signatures or personal notes.

A different authorized staff member checks the recorded choice and the exact term. Confirming extends the end date and preserves the original coverage start. If the member changes their mind before confirmation, withdraw the pending choice. If the member record moved to Trash, open **Review in-person cases** from Memberships Trash to withdraw the pending choice. If a saved response is interrupted, check the saved decision or retry the exact action.

Staff record what they observed in person. The workflow creates no online account, directory choice, email or payment record. The software checks the staff accounts and the term they reviewed. Your organization's in-person process establishes the member's identity.
