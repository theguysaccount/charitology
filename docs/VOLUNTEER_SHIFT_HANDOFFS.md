# Volunteer shift handoffs

A handoff keeps the confirmed place in place while two volunteers and a staff member review a change. It applies to a future, approved, whole shift and two existing linked volunteer requests. The person currently booked can agree to leave, and the first eligible person on the saved waitlist can agree to join. Staff make the final change. Until that succeeds, the roster stays as it was.

1. Open **Volunteers**, choose the shift and open its participation roster. Use **Review shift handoff options** on a confirmed volunteer's row. You need permission to read and edit Volunteers for the whole workspace.
2. Read the shift and the proposed replacement. The app checks the first saved waitlist place against the roster as if the original place were released. If the first place cannot take the shift, the app does not offer a later place through this workflow.
3. Use **Prepare this handoff offer**. Each volunteer signs in to **My requests**, opens their linked request and reads their own handoff choice. Each person can accept or withdraw their own choice while the offer is open. The offer closes within ten minutes, or when the shift starts if that comes first. Opening an offer does not send email.
4. After both volunteers accept, staff review both effects and confirm. The server checks both accounts, the current approval, the waitlist, schedule conflicts and capacity again. It saves the release and replacement together. If the response is interrupted, use **Retry** to check the same decision.

The handoff preserves the number of confirmed places. It does not transfer an account, bypass independent approval, accept a group booking, or change a check-in. Each volunteer sees their own choice and shift details. The other volunteer's identity stays with the authorized staff roster.

Saved handoffs remain readable as inactive history in a workspace backup. A restore keeps the history and does not recreate sign-ins, offers, choices or confirmed places. The original shift, assignments, Contacts and Forms records needed to read saved history cannot be sent to Trash or disconnected from that history. Contact redaction, request erasure, unlinking, ordinary cancellation and publication withdrawal still work. A saved choice cannot be used after expiry. The choice store removes expired rows in bounded batches when new choices are recorded.

For Contact reconciliation, handoff history supplies a typed dependency for the Contacts it actually involves. It does not create a dependency for unrelated Contacts or treat archived consent as current authority. An archive that includes another unsupported history family still needs review before reconciliation.

For self-hosting, install the additive migration in `services/charity-os/scripts/migrations/2026-11-09-volunteer-substitution.sql` after the application and sign-in tables exist. Use the dedicated choice grants and verifier generator for the same schema and constrained runtime role, in the migration transaction. For a non-superuser database owner, use `managedDatabaseOwner: true` so the temporary role transfer grant is removed. Enable `OS_VOLUNTEER_SUBSTITUTIONS_ENABLED=1` after the database checks pass. The hosted and development entry points expect the default verifier name and schema. Keep the new data and its grants in place when hiding the workflow, so existing history stays readable.
