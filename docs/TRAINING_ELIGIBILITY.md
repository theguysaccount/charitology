# Site orientation for a volunteer shift

Use **Participation** to open one future, approved, whole Volunteers shift with one place and a named program. An authorized owner or administrator chooses **Review site orientation requirement** and proposes the requirement. A different authorized owner or administrator reviews the same approved shift and the organization’s orientation register before approving it.

On the participation ledger, choose **Review site orientation** for an individual volunteer place. Check the exact person and result in the organization’s authorized register. Record its revision, status and any completion and validity dates. Keep original training documents in the register. For a complete result, the date range must cover the whole shift in its approved time zone. Unknown, withdrawn, expired or stale evidence returns the place to review.

Confirm or Promote checks the current requirement and evidence again when it saves. Existing waitlist and cancellation controls remain available. Use a separate individually reviewed place for a shift that requires orientation.

A workspace backup preserves the shift’s requirement as review needed. The organization’s register retains individual results. A restored requirement stays inactive for booking until the workspace can verify it again through a supported review. The first workflow is for staff-created whole-shift places on one managed shift per workspace.
