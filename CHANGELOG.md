# Changelog

## October 26, 2026: own-request participant portal

- Added a separate participant portal requiring both a private receipt and a matching verified email, with explicit account-linking consent. Signing in alone discovers no requests or team membership.
- Added own-only pagination and details, revision-checked pending corrections with renewed purpose consent, withdrawal, bounded answer erasure and permanent account unlinking. Submitted identity cannot be transferred; backup imports restore no participant access grants.
- Kept receipt capabilities out of login emails, callbacks, query parameters and browser storage. [The participant acceptance record](docs/PARTICIPANT_PORTAL.md) preserves the planning, threat model, test results and deployment status without claiming actual participant adoption or human review.

## October 26, 2026: public participation in Charity OS

- Added public operational forms from independently approved, published revisions, with explicit purpose consent, typed answers, bounded private queues and receipt links for status and withdrawal.
- Added administrator review, explicit conversion into draft contacts and optional event registrations or volunteer assignments, publication withdrawal and answer erasure. Requests do not confirm bookings, move money or subscribe anyone to email.
- Extended private organization backups to preserve intake data while removing receipt/network credentials. Restored forms stay closed, receipt access stays disabled and prior reviews grant no authority. Erasure does not remove separately prepared records or historical backup copies.

- Added a dated IRS identity snapshot of 1,975,975 unique EINs with explicit-request local search, leading-zero preservation, code/source exports and raw/compressed/decoded integrity checks. It is separate from 16 editorial profiles and does not rate charities.
- Preserved unknown donor depth across totals, exports and Wrapped; added saved-work recovery controls. Corrected forecast cutoffs, resolution provenance, annulment and ambiguous-record handling.
- Published the exact [66-item acceptance register](docs/MEGA_LIST_ACCEPTANCE.md). Later public-participation workflows, real connector accounts, pilots, independent review, licensed payments and institutional operations retain explicit gates. AI-provider activation is deferred.
