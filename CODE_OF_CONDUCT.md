# Code of Conduct

Charitology studies systems, not people’s hearts. We hold our own community to the same principle.

## What we expect

- **Argue with claims, not people.** Challenge evidence, reasoning and framing as hard as you like. Never attack a person’s character, identity or motives.
- **Assume good faith.** Most people in the charity system, and in this project, are trying to do good.
- **Bring sources.** Disagreements are settled by evidence and stated openly when they can’t be.
- **Make room.** Welcome newcomers, people from every moral and religious tradition, people the charity system serves, and people who disagree with Charitology.
- **Protect privacy.** Never post personal information about anyone, including the people charities serve.

## What isn’t acceptable

Harassment, insults, slurs, threats, sexualized content, doxxing, sustained disruption, and pressuring anyone to give money.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block participants who don’t follow this code. To report a problem privately, use GitHub’s *Report content* feature on the comment or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
